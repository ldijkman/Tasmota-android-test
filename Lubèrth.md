The page is online. On Android it opens the app straight away. If the app doesn't open, it shows a big green Open the app button, and if the app isn't installed, that button downloads it. On a PC it says the app is for Android.

To paste into GitHub Discussions:

[Open the app](https://ldijkman.github.io/Tasmota-android-test/open.html)

You can also open a screen directly:

https://ldijkman.github.io/Tasmota-android-test/open.html#thermostat opens the Heating screen https://ldijkman.github.io/Tasmota-android-test/open.html#tiles opens Tiles https://ldijkman.github.io/Tasmota-android-test/open.html#scenes opens Scenes 

When someone taps the link in GitHub, Chrome may only show the button instead of opening the app at once. One more tap on the button opens it.
